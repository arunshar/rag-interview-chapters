## What every study unit contains

1. A title and one-sentence purpose.
2. A `TL;DR` with plain-language takeaways.
3. A sustained explanatory story or analogy.
4. A three-column Decoder table.
5. Core mechanics, failure modes, and applicable cost or complexity.
6. Recreated diagrams and tables.
7. A Whiteboard pack with drawing order and a roughly 100 word script.
8. Interview traps with concise answers.
9. Key numbers when the source provides them.
